# Anti-Slop Reference

Source: Leo/SKILL.md (STRONG_HEURISTIC)

## Purpose
Prevents unmotivated glassmorphism, decorative gradients, and card spam.

## Guidelines
1. Avoid decorative background gradients or random glass effects without product justification.
2. Prevent unnecessary card wrappers around simple text or inline elements.
3. Eliminate arbitrary visual decoration that does not improve readability or task completion.
